What can I create with Forma?

This concept explores an AI website builder for portfolios, product launches, and online stores. The working demo uses three curated local templates to bring the experience to life. Choose a direction, preview the result, and export a real HTML file.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding is needed to explore the demo. Write a brief, choose a style, and open your preview. If you like to get into the details, your downloaded HTML is ready to edit in any text editor.

Can I take my website with me?

Yes. Every demo preview includes an Export HTML button. The download contains your page, its styles, and the visual artwork in a single file that opens directly in a browser.

Is this connected to an AI model?

This is an interactive frontend concept. Prompt matching and preview generation happen locally with curated templates; there is no live AI connection, account system, payment service, or publishing backend. Your brief stays in your browser.
